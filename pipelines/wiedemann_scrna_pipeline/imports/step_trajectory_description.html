Computes pseudotime and inferres trajectories as well as PAGA.

Output
A single diffusion_pseudotime_umap.svg plot visualising the diffusion pseudotime is generated. Per clustering the following files are generated:

  • monocle_cellstate_{number of clusters}.svg: UMAP visualising inferred cell states.
  • monocle_dge_{number of clusters}.svg: UMAPs of Monocle3 inferred differentially expressed genes.
  • monocle_differential_gene_expression_{number of clusters}.csv: Tabular representation Monocle3 inferred differentially expressed genes.
  • monocle_principal_{number of clusters}.svg: Principle plot ontop the UMAP showing clusters.
  • monocle_pseudotime_{number of clusters}.svg: Principle plot ontop the UMAP showing pseudotime.
  • paga_compare_{number of clusters}.svg: UMAP showing clusters next to the respective PAGA representation.
  • paga_graph_{number of clusters}.svg: Raw PAGA representation of the clustering.
